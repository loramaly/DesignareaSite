How the dispatch agent works
Three levels of autonomy, set by design:

Acts alone - routine, reversible work the team rules already cover: queue triage, status sync, the morning digest. It acts, and logs every step.

Suggests - assignments carry weight. The agent proposes with its confidence and its reasoning, and the admin approves in one click.

Steps back - under the threshold it never guesses. It flags the job, says what it is not sure about, and waits.
