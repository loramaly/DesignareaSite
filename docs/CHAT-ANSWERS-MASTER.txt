#01 Q: How do you design AI agents?
    A: "I approach agentic design through four core principles. Control & Boundaries: the agent automates complex logic, but the human retains decision authority - especially for high-stakes or irreversible actions. Explainability & Trust: instead of a black box, the system clearly presents its reasoning - why a specific decision or route was generated, based on real-time constraints like weather, traffic or user preferences. Latency & Uncertainty: clear visual feedback for processing times, with confidence indicators so users know when a suggestion needs quick verification. Graceful Override: a great agent UX makes it seamless to adjust, tweak or completely override suggestions without breaking the workflow."
